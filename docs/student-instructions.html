How to Check Out Equipment

Everything that leaves the shelf gets scanned out. It takes about 10 seconds.

1

Scan your student ID

Hold the barcode on your ID card under the scanner until it beeps.

No card? Type your ID number and press Enter.

2

Scan the item's label

Scan the QR code on the item, or on its bin or drawer.

No label or it won't scan? Type the item's name and click it in the list.

3

Enter how many & confirm

Type how many you're taking (or leave it at 1), then press Enter or click ✓ Confirm Checkout.

Add a note (project, class period) if your teacher asks for one.

✓ Look for the green message with the item and your ID number in the bottom corner of the screen. No message means it didn't save, so ask your teacher.

↩ Returning items

Give items back to your teacher (or the Returns bin). Don't put them straight back on the shelf. It stays checked out to you until your teacher checks it in.

Supplies (screws, zip ties…)

Same 3 steps. Supplies that get used up don't need to be returned. Scan them so we know when to reorder.

⚠ Broken or damaged?

Tell your teacher when you return it. Reporting damage is fine; hiding it is not.

Screen says…

  • Out of stock: all of them are checked out. Ask your teacher.
  • No item matches: type the name instead, and tell your teacher the label needs replacing.
  • Not on Check Out: click Check Out at the top.

Rules

  • Only scan your own ID. Whatever is checked out to your ID is your responsibility.
  • Don't touch the 🔒 Admin button.
  • Screen already shows someone else's ID? Click Change and scan yours.
  • Changed your mind halfway through? Just walk away; it clears itself after 2 minutes.
